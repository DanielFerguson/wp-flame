# Refund policy draft

Status: commercial draft for review before checkout publication.

The recommended initial offer is a 14-day refund period for a first annual software purchase. A customer may request a refund within 14 calendar days of the initial purchase if WP Flame does not meet their needs.

Before publication, the seller must finalize:

- Legal seller name, business address, contact channel, governing law, currency, tax treatment, and payment processor.
- Whether renewals have a separate notice/grace/refund rule.
- Treatment of separately delivered expert services once work has begun.
- Statutory consumer rights that cannot be excluded in the buyer's jurisdiction.
- Abuse/fraud handling and the method/timing of refund settlement.

The final policy must not limit non-excludable consumer guarantees. It must be shown before payment, linked from renewal notices, and kept consistent with checkout configuration. This draft is product-operating material, not legal advice.
