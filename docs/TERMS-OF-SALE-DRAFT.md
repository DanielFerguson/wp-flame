This draft describes the intended transaction. It is not legal advice and does not replace non-excludable consumer rights.

## Founding offer

The proposed RC offer is `$99 [CURRENCY]` for one year of:

- Access to the directly supplied WP Flame release-candidate packages for up to five supported production sites.
- Checksum, release-note, compatibility, and rollback information for supplied candidate builds.
- Founder onboarding and the support scope published at the time of purchase.
- Participation in the release-candidate feedback programme, subject to separately recorded site authorization and optional research/publication consents.

This is a validation offer, not a promise that every RC feature or the future public product will remain unchanged. Material changes to what the customer purchased require clear notice and any remedy required by applicable law.

## Software license and commercial entitlement

The supplied plugin code is licensed under GNU GPL version 2. The site count defines the deployments for which the seller promises design-partner delivery, onboarding, and support; it does not remove rights granted by the GPL to use, study, modify, or redistribute the supplied GPL code.

The RC has no embedded license check or automatic WP Flame update service. Payment records the commercial support and delivery relationship outside the plugin. Cancellation, expiry, or a refund does not remotely disable installed code or delete locally stored trace data.

WP Flame names, logos, website content, or separately delivered services are not granted additional rights merely because the plugin code is GPL; the final terms must state their applicable ownership and permitted use accurately.

## Product boundary

WP Flame observes supported WordPress execution and presents performance evidence. It is not:

- A complete PHP call-stack, infrastructure, browser, or Core Web Vitals profiler.
- A guarantee that every database query, network call, callback, plugin, theme, or request is captured.
- An automatic optimization, repair, availability, incident-response, or backup service.
- A vulnerability scanner or security-monitoring service.
- A guarantee of a specific speed improvement or business result.

Capability, completeness, mode, environment, comparison, and benchmark limitations form part of the product description.

## Customer responsibilities

The customer must:

- Have authority to install and operate the plugin on each site and comply with applicable privacy, employment, hosting, and data rules.
- Maintain appropriate backups, change controls, restoration access, and site administration.
- Start with the documented conservative mode and use Deep only as a bounded diagnostic.
- Review redacted exports and support bundles before sharing them and never send credentials, cookies, license keys, raw secrets, or unrelated personal data.
- Independently assess and test remediation advice before deploying a site change.
- Promptly report suspected WP Flame-caused fatal errors, data loss, privacy exposure, unbounded storage, or material regressions and narrow/stop capture where reasonably necessary.

Buying the offer does not authorize the seller to access a site, trace, export, meeting recording, testimonial, logo, screenshot, or case-study material. Each applicable permission is separate.

## Delivery, compatibility, and updates

The seller supplies the identified immutable ZIP and SHA-256 through `[DELIVERY CHANNEL]`. The customer should verify the checksum before installation. Supported PHP, WordPress, database, browser, hosting, and plugin-stack conditions are those on the linked compatibility page for that exact version.

RC updates are manually supplied during the programme. The seller must state whether an update is required, recommended, or optional, and provide material changes and rollback information. The customer controls installation unless a separate written service is agreed.

No term should promise uninterrupted availability of checkout, support, hosting, WordPress, third-party services, or customer sites.

## Support and separately scoped services

Software support is limited to the published support scope and response target. It does not include third-party code repair, emergency restoration, unrestricted consulting, or guaranteed resolution. Expert performance reviews or implementation work require a separate written scope, price, authorization, and treatment of intellectual property and confidentiality.

## Payment, renewal, cancellation, and refund

Complete before publication:

- Currency, tax-inclusive/exclusive display, invoice details, payment timing, and processor/merchant-of-record responsibilities.
- Whether the founding purchase renews automatically. If it does, renewal price, notice, cancellation method, and timing must be prominent before payment.
- The final refund policy, initially proposed as 14 calendar days for the first annual software purchase.
- Treatment of renewals, chargebacks, fraud, and separately delivered services after work begins.

Refund and cancellation terms cannot exclude remedies or guarantees that applicable law makes non-excludable.

## Data and confidentiality

The plugin stores trace data on the customer's WordPress site by default and originates no WP Flame telemetry, license, analytics, or update request in the RC. The published privacy notice must describe the plugin's local data, optional sensitive settings, retention, export, erasure, and uninstall behavior.

If the customer asks the seller to review trace material or access a site, the parties must separately record purpose, scope, authorized people, transfer method, retention/deletion, confidentiality, incident contact, and any legally required data-processing terms before access.

## Warranty, liability, governing law, and disputes

These clauses require jurisdiction-specific drafting. Do not insert a blanket warranty disclaimer, liability exclusion/cap, indemnity, governing-law clause, arbitration requirement, or shortened claim period without qualified review of business and consumer transactions in the relevant jurisdictions.

The final text must preserve non-excludable statutory guarantees and remedies and must not misdescribe the evidentiary, diagnostic nature of the software.

Before payment, link the final:
