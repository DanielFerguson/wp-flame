# Paid design-partner gate for M7

Status: required external evidence; not yet passed.

Full Pro edition, licensing, checkout, and authenticated-updater implementation must not begin until this gate records real paid behavior. Survey interest alone is insufficient.

The accepted RC channel is the single directly supplied package in `docs/architecture/RC-DISTRIBUTION.md`. Manual billing or a hosted checkout may record payment, but the plugin itself has no entitlement or update-service dependency. This is the offer being validated, not an implemented Community/Pro split.

## Reversible founding offer

- $99/year for up to five production sites, with local/staging sites excluded from activation counts.
- Founder onboarding and a weekly issue review during the RC period.
- Each participant brings at least one real slow dynamic WordPress workflow.
- Site authorization, trace review, research notes, testimonials, and case-study permission are separate choices.
- Expiry hypothesis: installed local functionality continues; updates, support, and connected services stop.

## Minimum evidence before architecture lock

- At least five completed paid transactions from the target agency/WooCommerce audience.
- At least three participants use WP Flame for a second incident or recurring workflow.
- A recorded ranking of monitoring, longer history, cohort comparison, reports, support, and fleet needs based on observed use.
- Measured onboarding/support time and at least one credible support-cost estimate per account tier.
- Written confirmation that Community still solves one complete diagnosis.
- A decision record accepting or revising the Pro boundary, price metric, activation rules, refund period, and license-expiry behavior.
